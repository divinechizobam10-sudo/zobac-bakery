About Zobac Bakery

Freshness, quality and joy in every bite.

OUR STORY

Where It All Began

Zobac Bakery was founded on 12 February 2022 by Mrs. Joy Nwachukwu, with a simple but meaningful goal: to bring freshly baked goodness, joy, and quality to every customer.

What began with a passion for baking has grown into a bakery dedicated to creating delicious bread, beautiful cakes, and delightful pastries for everyday moments and special celebrations.

WHAT WE DO

Made Fresh With Care

We specialize in freshly baked bread, beautifully crafted cakes, and delicious pastries made with quality ingredients and prepared with care.
